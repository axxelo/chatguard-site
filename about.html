Built where markets meet compliance.

ChatGuard is a communications surveillance and desk-intelligence platform — a product of AXTRADE SAS. We built it because the firms we knew were storing millions of conversations and getting nothing back but risk. So we made the same record do two jobs at once.

Our approach

Two engines, one record.

Every message we capture is encrypted once and read two ways: as desk intelligence that makes traders faster, and as compliance and HR tooling that keeps the firm safe. That dual use is the whole idea — surveillance you'd want even if the regulator never called.

Why us

Expertise on both sides.

We combine legal, compliance and markets knowledge with modern engineering, so the product fits the regulation and the trading floor. Deploy it inside your own perimeter, priced to scale with the firm, supported by people who've sat on both sides of the desk.
