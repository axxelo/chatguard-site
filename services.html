Home / Services

One captured record. Two sets of services.

Everything ChatGuard does sits on a single encrypted archive of your communications. From there we run two service lines — desk intelligence that makes traders faster, and compliance & HR tooling that keeps the firm safe — plus the deployment and support to stand it all up.

Service line 01 · Desk Intelligence

Make your Bloomberg chat work for the desk.

For Axtrade's traders, desk heads and COO. We mine the conversations your team is already having and turn them into structured, queryable productivity data.

01

RFQ & quote extraction

Our NLP reads free-text Bloomberg chat and identifies requests for quotes, the prices shown, and the fills — structuring shorthand like "25m EUR/USD where are you" into a clean, searchable trade-conversation book.

Inputs: IB chat, persistent roomsOutput: structured RFQ ledgerReal-time
02

Counterparty & flow analytics

See which counterparties you talk to most, average response times, win/hit rates by name and instrument, and where flow is quietly drying up. Spot the relationship that's gone cold before it shows up in the P&L.

Hit-rate by counterpartyResponse-time trackingFlow decay alerts
03

Open follow-up tracking

"Will confirm by 10", "let me check and revert" — every implicit commitment is detected and tracked as an open item until it's closed, so nothing slips between a busy desk and the next trade.

Auto-detected commitmentsAgeing alertsDesk & individual views
04

Sentiment & tone analysis

Score the tone of client and counterparty conversations over time. Surface mounting frustration, momentum shifts and at-risk relationships early enough to do something about them.

Per-relationship trendEscalation signalsML-driven
05

Unified search & export

Query every conversation across chat, email and voice in one place, then export a structured slice — by desk, person, counterparty, instrument or date — to your BI tools, data warehouse or a board pack.

Service line 02 · Compliance & HR

Prove conduct. Protect the firm.

For compliance officers, HR teams and business owners at regulated firms. The surveillance, archiving and investigation tooling you need to meet your obligations — and demonstrate that you have.

Archiving

Immutable archive & retrieval

Every message retained 5+ years, tamper-evident and timestamped. Reconstruct any thread for an audit or legal request in seconds.

Surveillance

Keyword & pattern alerts

Custom alerts flag market abuse, off-channel talk and misconduct. Machine learning surfaces patterns of behaviour humans miss.

Governance

Policy enforcement

Hold conversations to your internal policies automatically, with educational nudges that reduce repeat breaches over time.

Reporting

One-click regulator reports

Generate audit packs for the FCA, FINRA or internal review on demand — the evidence assembled and formatted for you.

Human Resources

HR investigation toolkit

Pull a full, documented evidence trail for a person or period. Identify recurring issues and intervene before they escalate.

Oversight

Compliance dashboard

One console for reviewers with role-based permissions, case management and a complete record of who reviewed what, when.
