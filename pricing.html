Pricing questions

How is it priced?

By channels in scope, number of seats and deployment model. Tell us your setup and we quote precisely — usually after a short scoping call.

Is there a trial?

Yes — a parallel run on your own data alongside your current tooling, so you can compare alert quality and desk metrics before committing.

Where is it hosted?

Inside your perimeter — self-host or your private cloud. Your communications are never sent to a third party.

Can I start with one engine?

Yes. Take Desk Intelligence or Compliance & HR alone, and add the other later on the same captured record.
